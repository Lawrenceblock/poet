{{< poem >}}
There is a room where no one speaks,
where the lamp keeps its small promise
and the dust learns the shape of light.

I have kept a chair for you there,
turned toward the window,
turned toward the years.

If you come, come quietly —
the walls are thin with listening,
and the night is almost whole.
{{< /poem >}}
