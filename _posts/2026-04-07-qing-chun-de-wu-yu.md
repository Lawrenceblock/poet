<div class="poem">


<br>


文/安然<br>
<br>

<div style="text-align: center;">

青春的物语<br>
停歇在你大眼睛的那束清亮之上，<br>
你为什么不……<br>
我久久退却，<br>
你的哀怨在记忆的阴影里<br>
再次照亮我。<br>
<br>
我不曾用甜言蜜语喂养过的早衰的时代，<br>
花朵般的吻<br>
不属于我。<br>
那时的底色是十英寸的单调与灰暗，<br>
饥馑的感觉并未远离，<br>
而恐惧<br>
以真理之名，<br>
站在黄绿色的卡车上在市区游荡。<br>
我们相逢的课桌，<br>
刻满我所不思议的语言。<br>
<br>
脱缰的绿意<br>
所误入的铅灰的一角，<br>
试图同古老的血液对话：<br>
“你是三桓之后！”<br>
槐花的笑声缀满斗兽场的虚空，<br>
在那个夏日，<br>
在那个笨拙的黎明。<br>
<br>
溃散<br>
如命数一样如约到来，<br>
拣选<br>
是众神对人的<br>
第一场行刑、<br>
第一次枪杀。<br>
你为什么将槐花掷向那张死亡之网？<br>
你知道：<br>
理智像狼群<br>
在黑暗里窥伺。<br>

</div>

<div style="text-align: center;">

**Youth's Tale**<br>
<br>
*by **An Ran** *<br>
<br>
</div>

<div style="text-align: center;">

Youth's tale<br>
comes to rest upon the clear brightness in your large eyes.<br>
Why didn’t you…<br>
I retreat for a long, long time,<br>
and your sorrow, in the shadows of memory,<br>
lights me up once again.<br>
<br>
<br>
I never fed the prematurely aged era with sweet words,<br>
flower-like kisses<br>
do not belong to me.<br>
The base color of those days was ten inches of monotony and gray,<br>
the feeling of famine never far away,<br>
and fear,<br>
in the name of truth,<br>
stood on yellow-green trucks, roaming the city streets.<br>
The desk where we met<br>
was carved with words I could never have imagined.<br>
<br>
<br>
The runaway greenness<br>
that strayed into this lead-gray corner,<br>
tried to converse with ancient blood:<br>
“You are a descendant of the Three Huans!”<br>
The laughter of locust flowers filled the void of the amphitheater,<br>
on that summer day,<br>
in that clumsy dawn.<br>
<br>
<br>
Disintegration<br>
arrived as punctually as fate.<br>
Selection<br>
was the gods’ first execution upon man,<br>
the first shooting.<br>
Why did you throw the locust flowers toward that net of death?<br>
You knew:<br>
<br>
reason is like a pack of wolves,<br>
lurking in the darkness.<br>

</div>
